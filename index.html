About me

I am a DPhil student in Mathematics at the University of Oxford, jointly supervised by Prof. Samuel Cohen and Prof. Christoph Reisinger. Before joining Oxford, I completed my undergraduate studies at the University of Liverpool, followed by a master’s degree at the University of Michigan.

My doctoral research, funded by CBOE, focuses on stochastic control and game theory in finance, particularly stochastic games between competing market makers in option markets with exchange-provided incentives. This work provides insights into how competition and incentive design shape liquidity provision, and how exchanges can design fees to improve market liquidity.
